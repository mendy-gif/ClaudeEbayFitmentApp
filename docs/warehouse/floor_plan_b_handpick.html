Storage plan for 17,000 items

  1. Main room, ~13,000. Move your existing shelving. You already own most of the 721 ft, so this is labor and anchors, not new racks.
  2. Office wing, ~2,500. Line one or two of the small rooms with the same shelving. Roughly 150 ft of face is enough. Photos share the wing.
  3. Outdoor containers, ~2,000. Two 40 ft high-cube containers on a gravel pad for doors, hoods, bumpers, wheels and slow movers. Door racks inside. Forklift loads them over the ramp.
  4. Upgrade later: two-tier pick module. A steel deck over the main block at 8 ft with 7 ft shelving on both levels. Doubles the face to about 1,040 ft in the same footprint, no ladders, fastest picking. Roughly 70 to 100k installed. Place the floor rows now so a deck can go over them without moving anything.

  2. Forklift: keep or buy one 3-wheel electric counterbalance, 4,000 lb. Works the ramp, the yard, containers, hulks and the heavy rack. Never enters a shelving aisle.
